# Changelog

## 0.1.0-beta.1 research preview

### Scientific scope

- Versioned seed-to-voxel and ROI-to-ROI resting-state workflows.
- One-sample and two-independent-group models with optional numeric covariates.
- Explicit contrasts, hypotheses, parameter decisions, evidence snapshots, QC
  decisions, sample versions, and scientific decisions.
- Public WU120, fibromyalgia, and UCLA CNP benchmark registrations.

### Saved-result scientific audit

- Audit actual model subjects, frozen matrix values, rank, residual degrees of
  freedom, condition and contrast; absent evidence blocks scientific currency.
- Export availability-aware QC, recover ART evidence with file hashes, and
  compute contiguous outliers on acquired-volume rows; retain human decisions.
- Extract exploratory univariate T evidence with explicit two-sided p-values,
  within-model BH families and pointwise ROI intervals checked against saved OLS.
- Add audit/statistics records to reproducibility reports with sample/output
  binding, pending items and a retrospective command that preserves originals.
- Synthetic integration and numerical tests cover these additions; fresh CONN
  validation and unsupported inference remain documented limits.

### Parameter-selection audit

- Complete both-workflow MCP and ZIP round-trip coverage, plus a mandatory
  PostgreSQL 16 CI job for migration, durable choices, rollback and ownership.
- Reject numeric text and input that overflow floating-point conversion.

- Expose all mapped observations, reasons for ineligibility, descriptive context
  alignment and missing methodological appraisal dimensions through MCP.
- Parse only unambiguous numeric values with expected units; preserve signs and
  reject nonfinite values, qualified component counts and contradictory bands.
- Freeze candidate/context/citation snapshots with hashes in atomic method
  decisions; detect altered evidence and changes to the recorded project context.
- Preserve legacy decisions as audit-unavailable; do not reinterpret historical
  benchmark choices or turn citation frequency into a recommendation.

### Validation additions — October 2026

- Added an official CONN source guide, machine-readable consultation index,
  release-pinned implementation links, and citation instructions for AI clients.
- Completed six full benchmark analyses after a three-participant smoke gate.
- Reviewed 55 unique participants and recalculated clinical second-level models
  for the final QC samples.
- Recovered the prospective WU120 PCC/default-mode positive control.
- Published machine-readable aggregate results and package hashes.
- Corrected second-level covariate aliasing and verified participant counts,
  design rank, and residual degrees of freedom.
- Added resilient-worker recovery for interrupted local attempts while preserving
  the durable remote job identity.
- Added scientist-facing method, QC, evidence-governance, data-governance,
  selector-evaluation, worked-example, glossary, and MCP reference material.

### Remaining research-preview limits

- Clinical benchmark subsets are underpowered for definitive paper replication.
- The evidence corpus is not a complete systematic review or meta-analysis.
- The parameter selector has demonstrated traceability and baseline concordance,
  not superiority over blinded experts or alternative pipelines.
- Participant-level derivatives and exact controlled sample manifests are not
  distributed through this public repository.
- Legacy QC exports require availability checking and verification of contiguous
  outliers from volume-indexed ART evidence.
